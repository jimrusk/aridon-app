# Aridon Revenue Channel Launch Kit

## Goal

Use external marketplaces as customer-acquisition channels while keeping Aridon's own storefront and recurring Business OS as the core brand. The objective is not to turn Aridon into a low-cost gig shop. The objective is to create multiple doors into the same value ladder:

1. Free business analysis
2. $198 Business Health Scan
3. $495 Growth Blueprint
4. $1,500 Executive Business Audit
5. $2,500 Acquisition Due-Diligence Package
6. $497/month Aridon Business OS

## Primary payment strategy

Use Stripe as Aridon's payment backbone. It reduces fragmentation, keeps the direct customer experience under the Aridon brand, and supports both fixed-price services and recurring subscriptions.

Marketplace-originated work should follow the marketplace's current payment and communication rules. Do not route a marketplace client off-platform when the platform requires the transaction to remain on-platform.

# 1. Upwork

## Positioning

Do not lead with generic "AI consultant." Lead with measurable business diagnosis, revenue recovery, acquisition analysis, and executive decision support.

## Profile headline

**AI Business Growth & Acquisition Analyst | Revenue Leaks, Automation, Due Diligence**

## Profile overview

Aridon helps owner-led businesses find where revenue, time, and opportunity are leaking and turns the findings into a prioritized action plan.

Typical engagements include business and website health scans, conversion analysis, revenue-recovery opportunities, AI automation planning, competitor review, 90-day growth roadmaps, and acquisition due diligence.

The work is designed for owners who want decision-ready output rather than another generic AI report. Each engagement is scoped around the real business, website, operating problem, or acquisition target.

Core packages include:
- Business Health Scan
- Growth Blueprint
- Executive Business Audit
- Acquisition Due-Diligence Package
- Ongoing AI executive operating support

No revenue outcome is guaranteed. Recommendations are intended to support owner decision-making and execution.

# 5. Lemon Squeezy

## Decision

Do not add Lemon Squeezy at launch. Stripe already supports the direct fixed-price and recurring Aridon offers. Adding a second direct checkout system creates duplicate products, reporting, customer records, refunds, and subscription management.

Revisit Lemon Squeezy only if Aridon later needs a merchant-of-record model for a large international digital-product catalog and the tax/compliance tradeoff justifies migration.

# 8. Operating rules

- Never promise a specific revenue increase.
- Never describe acquisition analysis as legal, tax, accounting, securities, or investment advice.
- Use the customer's real website or acquisition target whenever possible.
- Keep the first paid engagement small enough to reduce buying friction.
- Ask for a recurring subscription only after the customer has seen useful output.
- Track channel, offer, close rate, average order value, subscription conversion, and churn.
- Double down on the channels that produce profitable customers, not merely clicks.
