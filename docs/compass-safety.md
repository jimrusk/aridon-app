# Compass evidence and approval rules

- Treat investment graphics, listing claims and seller statements as leads to verify, not established facts.
- Separate user-entered, seller-provided, public-source and independently verified data.
- Do not represent projected returns as guaranteed.
- Require property-specific evidence before asserting NOI, occupancy, recession resistance, appreciation, profitability or owner income.
- Do not commit to price, LOI terms, financing, proof-of-funds representations, exclusivity, closing dates or contracts without owner approval.
- Preserve source provenance for material financial and property facts.
