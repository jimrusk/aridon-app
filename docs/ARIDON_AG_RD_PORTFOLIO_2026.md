# Aridon Ag R&D Portfolio 2026

## Purpose

Create a field-first agricultural R&D program for Southwest farms and ranches. The portfolio is designed around water scarcity, farm profitability, resilience, measurable field outcomes and the commercialization gap between laboratory research and farmer adoption.

The portfolio intentionally separates **design targets** from **measured performance**. Hardware, agronomic and economic claims must be earned through instrumented field trials and independent validation.

## Five flagship programs

### 1. Aridon Water Independence System

**Goal:** Coordinate every available farm water source through one owner-controlled decision layer.

Inputs may include wells, rain capture, recycled water, brackish water, municipal water, atmospheric water generation, tanks, water quality, crop demand, energy price and weather.

Primary outputs:
- Water Security Days
- Cost per usable gallon
- Groundwater gallons avoided
- Storage utilization
- Source mix by day and crop zone

Research question: Can integrated water-source management reduce groundwater dependence and operating risk without harming crop performance or farm margin?

### 2. AWG-1000 Gen 3 Southwest

**Goal:** Develop and test a hybrid atmospheric water research platform for hot, low-humidity conditions.

Candidate operating modes:
- Direct condensation in favorable dew-point conditions
- Desiccant moisture capture during lower humidity
- Thermal regeneration using verified solar or waste-heat sources
- Schedule optimization based on weather, tank level and energy price

Primary validation endpoints:
- Liters per day by weather band
- kWh per liter
- Water quality
- Gallons per dollar
- Maintenance burden
- Desiccant cycle life

No production or efficiency target should be presented as validated until field testing confirms it.

### 3. RootIQ Precision Water

**Goal:** Replace timer-first irrigation with root-zone evidence.

Inputs:
- Soil moisture and/or tension at multiple depths
- Weather forecast
- Evapotranspiration
- Crop stage
- Rain probability
- Pump and valve status
- Irrigation and nutrient event history

Primary endpoints:
- Gallons per acre
- Root-zone stress hours
- Pump kWh
- Yield per acre
- Nutrient loss indicators
- Profit per acre

### 4. Farm Profit Digital Twin

**Goal:** Optimize farm economics, not yield alone.

The model combines:
- Acreage and crop/livestock mix
- Water and energy
- Labor
- Seed and fertilizer
- Equipment and maintenance
- Insurance
- Financing
- Storage and logistics
- Contracts and commodity price assumptions

Outputs:
- Net profit per acre
- Cash requirement
- Downside case
- Payback period
- Water cost per acre
- Forecast error after actual results arrive

Every scenario must expose assumptions and uncertainty.

### 5. Aridon Farm R&D Network

**Goal:** Create a repeatable path from research concept to instrumented farm trial.

Workflow:
1. Researcher submits technology, trial protocol and required site conditions.
2. Aridon identifies candidate farms without exposing private operating data.
3. Farmer chooses whether to participate and what data may be shared.
4. Baseline data is collected before the intervention.
5. Trial data follows a common evidence schema.
6. Results can be private, partner-only, anonymized or public according to agreement.
7. Successful technologies advance to larger trials.

Primary endpoints:
- Time to field
- Cost per validated trial
- Farmer participation and retention
- Replicable outcome rate
- Technologies advanced to the next stage

## Five expansion programs

### Soil Water Battery
Research porous mineral or ceramic root-zone structures that temporarily store irrigation water and release it as surrounding soil dries. Test usable water capacity, release curve, evaporation reduction, crop response, durability and installed cost.

### Nutrient Independence
Combine soil demand, manure, wastewater nutrient recovery and precision application to reduce purchased fertilizer. Test purchased N-P-K avoided, nutrient recovery efficiency, yield, runoff risk and net savings.

### Agricultural Early Warning
Combine permissioned farm observations with public weather and agricultural data to detect emerging pest, disease, drought and water-supply patterns. Test warning lead time, false-alert rate, farmer action rate and avoided loss.

### Livestock Water + Heat Sentinel
Combine trough level, water temperature, ambient heat, forecast and animal movement to identify water or heat stress earlier. Test alert lead time, response time and livestock-water availability.

### Farm Energy-Water Microgrid
Coordinate grid electricity, solar, batteries, pumps, treatment, AWG and water storage. Research when it is economically better to store electricity versus produce and store water.

## Southwest Water-Secure Farm Demonstration

Use a three-arm trial where site conditions are comparable enough to produce meaningful evidence.

### Arm A: Baseline control
Instrument the existing practice without optimizing it.

### Arm B: RootIQ precision
Add root-zone sensing, weather inputs and irrigation decision support.

### Arm C: Integrated water-secure farm
Add RootIQ, Water Independence System, storage and AWG research equipment. Add energy-water optimization as instrumentation permits.

## Common measurement package

Collect at minimum:
- Water withdrawals by source
- Water delivered by zone
- Water quality where relevant
- Soil moisture/tension
- Weather and evapotranspiration
- Pump and treatment energy
- Labor time
- Crop or livestock output
- Marketable yield where applicable
- Input costs
- Maintenance burden
- Net profit per acre or per production unit

## Evidence rules

1. Measured data and modeled data must never be displayed as if they are the same thing.
2. Baselines must be captured before intervention.
3. Sensor calibration and missing-data periods must be recorded.
4. Farmer data rights and sharing permissions must be explicit.
5. Negative results remain results and should not be buried.
6. Economic performance is part of technical performance.
7. Independent validation should be used before broad performance claims or commercialization.

## Partner presentation

### Opening

Aridon is proposing a Southwest field R&D platform that combines water-source coordination, precision irrigation, atmospheric water research, farm economics and a standardized field-trial network.

### What we are asking a research partner to do

- Review the engineering and measurement protocols
- Help identify a suitable pilot crop/site and control design
- Establish calibration and data-quality requirements
- Independently validate the results
- Help determine which technologies merit larger trials

### What we are asking a farm partner to do

- Provide a real operating environment
- Approve what data is collected and shared
- Help define practical maintenance and workflow requirements
- Evaluate whether the technology is useful, affordable and understandable

### What we are asking a funding or policy partner to do

- Support instrumented field validation rather than unverified scaling
- Fund measurement, engineering, equipment and farmer participation
- Help create a repeatable path from successful trial to broader deployment

**Aridon Southwest Water-Secure Farm R&D Demonstration**

Subtitle: **Field evidence for water resilience, precision agriculture and farmer profitability.**

## North-star metric

A technology is not ready because it looks advanced. It is ready when verified evidence shows that it improves one or more of the following without creating an unacceptable burden elsewhere:

**water saved + inputs reduced + risk reduced + farmer profit improved**
