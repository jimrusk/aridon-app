# Aridon Ag + Farm Bureau
## Proposed Member Technology Pilot

**Positioning:** Boost Sales. Improve Crops. Control Payroll. With Aridon.

### Executive Summary
Aridon proposes a Farm Bureau member technology pilot built to help farmers and ranchers improve profitability and simplify the business side of agriculture. Aridon Ag combines business intelligence, sales, crop economics, payroll and labor visibility, inputs and inventory, equipment, documents, and water-resilience intelligence in one AI-assisted operating layer.

The program is intentionally designed to complement existing accounting, field-management, payroll, and precision-agriculture systems. Producers should not have to replace tools that already work. Aridon can become the decision layer that turns information from those systems into prioritized actions.

### Why Farm Bureau
Farm Bureau organizations already create member value through preferred pricing and partner benefits. Aridon Ag extends that model into an operational technology benefit focused directly on producer economics.

### What Members Receive
- Aridon Ag command dashboard
- Sales and buyer pipeline visibility
- Crop and field profitability tracking
- Payroll, overtime, and labor-cost intelligence
- Input-cost and inventory management
- Equipment and work-order visibility
- Document and reporting center
- AI Farm Advisor with prioritized recommendations
- Water-use and drought-resilience intelligence roadmap
- Guided onboarding and member support

### Proposed Pilot
**Duration:** 90 days  
**Initial cohort:** 25-50 volunteer producer members  
**Farm types:** mix of crop, livestock, specialty, and diversified operations where practical  
**Member offer:** preferred pilot pricing or sponsored access to be negotiated with Farm Bureau  
**Support:** Aridon-led onboarding, training, and office hours

### Pilot Outcomes
Aridon will measure agreed operational indicators such as time saved on reporting, sales opportunities surfaced, labor-cost opportunities identified, input/inventory visibility, producer engagement, and member satisfaction. Financial improvements will be reported only where supported by producer data and appropriate consent.

### Partnership Options
1. **Member Benefit:** Farm Bureau members receive preferred Aridon Ag pricing.
2. **Sponsored Pilot:** Farm Bureau sponsors a defined member cohort for 90 days.
3. **Statewide Program:** Successful pilot expands to a statewide member offering.
4. **Multi-State / National Path:** Proven state programs can become a template for additional Farm Bureau organizations.

### Why Aridon Is Different
Aridon is not positioned as a replacement bookkeeping package. It is an AI-powered business operating layer that connects the economics of the farm to the work happening in the field. The roadmap also joins farm profitability with water resilience, drought exposure, and future verified water-production intelligence.

### Proposed Next Step
Hold a 30-minute working session with Farm Bureau membership/member-benefit and producer leadership. Select a pilot cohort, agree on success metrics, finalize member pricing, and launch a 90-day proof-of-value program.
