# Alternative Housing property diligence checklist

Before Aridon ranks a specific property, collect and verify:

- Asking price and proposed structure
- Trailing 12-month revenue and expenses
- Tax returns / financial statements where available
- Current rent roll and collections
- Physical and economic occupancy
- Site/unit mix and ownership model
- Utility ownership, billing and capacity
- Deferred maintenance and near-term capex
- Zoning and permitted uses
- Licenses and code compliance
- Property taxes and insurance
- Existing debt and assumability
- Available lender terms
- Seller financing, staged payments or earn-out structure if offered
- Debt service coverage
- Cash required at closing
- Post-debt owner cash flow
- Downside occupancy and expense scenarios
- Local supply, demand and comparable communities

No return, recession-resistance, occupancy, profitability or appreciation claim should be treated as established until supported by property- and market-specific evidence.
