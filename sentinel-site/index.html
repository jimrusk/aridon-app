Security without surrendering the steering wheel.

Layered monitoring plus explicit business controls keeps a false positive from automatically shutting down the company.

01 • ENDPOINT

Know what changed

Heartbeat, configurable file-integrity monitoring, process snapshots, local alerts, and evidence records.

02 • INCIDENT COMMAND

One response room

Prioritize incidents, record decisions, assign owners, and preserve an audit trail for IT, management, counsel, insurers, and responders.

03 • HUMAN OVERRIDE

Containment with judgment

High-impact response actions can require a named human approver with emergency override and reason logging.

04 • EVIDENCE

Preserve what matters

Capture timestamps, hashes, endpoint context, and response history for investigation.

05 • ESCALATION

Structured outside response

Prepare incident packages for counsel, insurers, MSSPs, or authorities. External reporting is human-controlled by default.

06 • ARIDON

Security meets operations

Connect Sentinel to Aridon OS for continuity planning, executive communications, recovery tasking, and business operations.
